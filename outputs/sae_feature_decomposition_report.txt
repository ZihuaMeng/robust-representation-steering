INTERPRETABILITY VERDICT
===============================================================================================

Case (b): DECOMPOSITION IS INTRINSICALLY DISTRIBUTED

Even the sparsest exact decomposition requires ~2433 significant
features. The robust delta is intrinsically distributed across many SAE features.
Top-20 captures only 13.6% of energy; top-50 captures 23.5%.
Interpretable steering would require trading off exact Rashomon coverage
for sparsity via L1-penalized robust optimization — a genuine algorithmic
extension, not merely a post-hoc analysis change.
