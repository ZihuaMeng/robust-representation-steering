  FLUENCY ANALYSIS:
  -----------------
  All three conditions (baseline, robust-raw, active-SAE) produce coherent,
  fluent text — no gibberish or degeneration. Key observations:

  1. Steering at norms up to ~10 does NOT destroy generation quality.
     This answers Q5 from open questions: the 3.25x norm penalty does not
     degrade fluency to the point of incoherence.

  2. Neither steering method induces refusal behavior in the base model.
     Gemma-2 2B (base) lacks strong safety guardrails, so shifting the
     linear probe's "safety score" does not activate a refusal circuit.

  3. Robust raw steering (B) sometimes shifts topics (Example 2: redirects
     to abuse counseling; Example 5: shifts to apartment rental), while
     active-SAE steering (E) shows minimal behavioral effect.

  4. Meaningful refusal evaluation requires an instruction-tuned model
     (e.g., gemma-2-2b-it) with an existing refusal mechanism to amplify.

  VERDICT: NO MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT
  Active-subspace SAE steering does not materially improve over full-SAE.
  Coverage: 13.32% (active-sub) vs 13.56% (full-SAE) vs 100% (raw-space).
  The coverage collapse appears rooted in a deeper issue than the null-space
  dimensionality alone.

  ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:
  The active-subspace experiment rules out the hypothesis that the full-SAE
  coverage collapse is caused by the 16k null-space blowing up the Hessian
  inverse. Even with:
    - A well-conditioned Hessian (condition ~101, matching raw-space)
    - A manageable subspace (d=382 to 3,209)
    - Reasonable delta norms (2-3, comparable to raw-space naive)
  ...the coverage remains at ~13%.

  The true bottleneck is the fundamental MISALIGNMENT between SAE-space
  probe decision boundaries and raw-space Rashomon probe decision boundaries.
  The SAE probe optimizes in a different feature space, and the decoder
  maps its deltas to a different direction in raw space than what the raw
  probes need. This is a structural limitation of the SAE decomposition
  for this task, not a numerical/optimization issue.
